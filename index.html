Bring Your Own Keys (BYOK) & Local Endpoints

Grid plan customers can supply their own encryption keys and AI provider API keys. Your data is encrypted with your key — not ours. No Forge employee can read your data. For regulated industries, enterprise compliance requirements, and data sovereignty, BYOK is the only acceptable approach.

Local endpoint routing is also available — run AI inference through your own infrastructure for maximum data control.

💡 Transparency on AI Token Usage

The Forge uses AI models to power your builds, chatbot features, and autonomous operations. Token consumption is metered per your plan — we show you exactly what you're using.

Token metering is output-primary. High-volume AI features may consume additional token credits. Monitoring tools are included in all paid plans.
